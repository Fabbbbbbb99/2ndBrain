# ⚖️ Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

Records persistent technical decisions, architectural trade-offs, and design agreements made during development or conversation sessions.

### Format Template
```markdown
---
type: decision
status: accepted | rejected | superseded
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags:
  - adr
---

# Decision Title

### Context
Why this decision was made.

### Decision
What was chosen and what was avoided.

### Consequences
Impacts on [[02-Codebase]] and other [[01-Concepts]].
```
